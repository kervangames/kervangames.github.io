Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kervan ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the mobile game Orbiter (the "Game"). By downloading or using the Game, you agree to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or phone number. The Game uses third-party services that may automatically collect certain information:

  • Usage and gameplay data — such as when a game session starts and ends, score, session duration, and progress through the tutorial.
  • Device and technical data — such as device model, operating system version, language, country, and app version.
  • Advertising identifiers — such as the Google Advertising ID, used to deliver and measure advertisements.

Third-Party Services

The Game uses the following third-party services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — used to understand how players use the Game so we can improve it. See the Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob — used to display advertisements, including interstitial ads and optional rewarded video ads. See the Google Advertising Policy.

Advertising

The Game displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Rewarded video ads are entirely optional; you may choose whether to watch them. Advertising partners may use device identifiers to show ads that are more relevant to you and to measure ad performance.

You can limit ad personalisation at any time through your device settings (for example, "Opt out of Ads Personalisation" in Android settings).

Local Storage

Your high score and settings preferences (sound, music, vibration) are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us.

Children

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Data Retention

Analytics data is retained by Google in accordance with its own retention policies. We do not maintain a separate database of user information.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your data, including the right to access, correct, or delete it. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.
